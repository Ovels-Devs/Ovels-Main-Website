# Ovels: free Discord bot and AI fitness coach by Ishan Jaiswal

Canonical page: https://ovels.in/ · Last updated: 2026-11-17

Ovels is the name Ishan Jaiswal builds software under. It is an independent studio run by a single developer. It started with a free Discord bot for moderation, tickets, music and giveaways, and an AI fitness coach app. What comes next could be a web app, a game or a simple tool, and it will live on ovels.in.

## Who is Ovels

Ovels is built and run by [Ishan Jaiswal](https://ishan.vip). It began with tools for an online community and is not tied to any one product or platform. Every release has to pass one test: would the developer still use it after the first week?

Principles:

- **Free at the core.** The parts people rely on every day cost nothing. Paid extras can come later, but the essentials of every product stay free.
- **Fast, focused tools.** Every product does one job and does it well. No bloat, no setup marathons.
- **Private by default.** Local first wherever that makes sense, and only the data a feature genuinely needs. Nothing extra, nothing sold.

## Projects

### 01. Ovels Bot: Discord bot and web dashboard

Site: https://bot.ovels.in · Status: bot online

The Discord bot that runs your server. A free Discord bot covering moderation, automod, tickets, giveaways, music, leveling and minigames. Set it up in minutes from a clean web dashboard: no config files and no command syntax to memorize, every module is a toggle.

- **Moderation:** warnings, automod and a mod log that writes itself.
- **Tickets:** structured support threads without the chaos.
- **Music:** playback straight in voice, tuned to stay smooth.
- **Minigames:** leveling and games that keep members around.

Key facts: 7 modules, 0 config files, online 24/7.

### 02. Ovels App: AI training and nutrition coach

Site: https://app.ovels.in · Status: coming soon to stores

One AI that reads every set and every meal. Scan a plate from a photo, get workout plans built around the equipment you actually own, and coach with an AI that already knows today's calories and last week's lifts. Local first: your data stays on your phone.

- **Meal scanner:** photo, text or barcode, macros logged in seconds.
- **Workout log:** AI plans with progressive overload tracking.
- **AI coach:** runs on your device when the hardware supports it.
- **Local first:** analytics only if you opt in, plus a full backup you control.
